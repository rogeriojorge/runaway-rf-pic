# L0 recommendations (2026-11-09)

Reading base: reference research docs plus targeted retrieval (Smilei docs, Angus 2025 abstract, DREAM docs, GitHub file lists). Items not retrieved are flagged in each file. Where a number is marked "computed", it was calculated in this lane (SI constants) and is an independent oracle.

## 1. Collisions
**Model:** equal-weight Nanbu-Perez relativistic COM pair scattering, uncapped Perez strength, Nanbu exact inverse-Langevin angle (or Smilei fit after statistical check), fixed lnL for validation runs, subcycles to keep s<0.1-1. **Weights:** Higginson 2020 for unequal weights (correct rate, conserves on average); **Alternative worth a comparison run:** Angus 2025 post-scatter restoration (exact per-cycle energy and momentum, relativistic claimed; full text unread).
**Targets:** per-pair momentum/energy conservation to roundoff (equal w); NRL beam relaxation, isotropization and T-equilibration (Smilei suite); Maxwellianization to Maxwell-Juttner; Beliaev-Budker/Braams-Karney drag as independent oracle; `E_c` friction scale 7.6e-4 V/m at n=1e18, lnL=15 (computed).
**Sources:** Perez 2012; Higginson 2020; Angus 2025 arXiv 2407.19151; Smilei collisions docs; WarpX `UpdateMomentumPerezElastic.H`; Smilei `src/Collisions/Collisions.cpp`.

## 2. Radiation reaction
**Model:** reduced LL (invariant drag, `g=1`), Boris + RR substep; cLL `g(chi)` only as the L5 option. At 20 MeV, 2 T, chi ~ 2e-8: classical regime, no quantum correction needed. **Alternatives:** full LL (plane-wave Di Piazza exact), Niel FP (pathwise noise), MC emission (not differentiable).
**Targets:** uniform-B cooling `gamma(t)=coth(t/tau_B+C)`, tau_B=1.290 s at 2 T; 20 MeV -> gamma 10 in 0.0973 s, -> 2 in 0.676 s (computed); pitch angle constant under reduced LL, `d ln|u|/dt = -gamma sin^2(theta)/tau_B`; convergence order in dt vs exact; Smilei cross-code numbers (a0=270, ~80% radiated; synchrotron energy error ~3e-9 [from docs]). Full-vs-reduced agreement O(1/gamma).
**Sources:** Vranic 2016; Di Piazza 2008; Niel 2018; Smilei radiation docs (`RadiationLandauLifshitz.cpp`, `RadiationNiel.cpp`); Bilbao-Silva 2023; Di Piazza 2018/2019 for LCFA limits.

## 3. Runaways and RF
**Model:** relativistic test-particle + PIC with collisions as above, avalanche knock-on via the large-angle collisions (Embreus 2018); reference fluid formula Hesslow 2019, not Rosenbluth-Putvinski alone. Whistler response checked against cold dispersion and cyclotron/Landau resonance, `omega - k_par v_par = -Omega/gamma`. **Alternatives:** quasilinear D_pp (Kennel-Engelmann, Guo 2025 slab), AORSA-KORC (Ghai 2026) trends.
**Targets:** E_c 7.6e-4 V/m (computed); DREAM `dream_avalanche` and CODE avalanche growth versus E/E_c: **numbers not retrieved; must be generated by running DREAM (L2/L4)**; whistler resonance momentum per run (analytic). No literature numbers for RF-driven RE decay were retrieved; do not claim any.
**Missing:** Aleynikova/Komar/Spong papers not located; do not cite without retrieval.
**Sources:** Connor-Hastie; Hesslow 2017/2018/2019; Landreman 2014 (CODE); Hoppe 2021 (DREAM docs, `examples/runaway*`); Liu 2018; Ghai 2026 (arXiv 2509.13271); Choudhury 2026.

## 4. Source power
**Model:** soft current-sheet antenna `J_ext` with Poynting-closure ledger: `P_src = -int J_ext.E`, `P_p = int J_p.E`, field energy plus boundary flux, RR loss separate; power-controlled drive (hold `<P_src>` fixed) for any minimum-energy optimization. **Alternative:** TF/SF plane-wave injection.
**Targets:** closure residual slope 2 in dt (leapfrog), roundoff for implicit; vacuum antenna `<P_src>` equals two-sided outgoing flux within 1e-3; collisionless cold-plasma whistler zero absorption.
**Sources:** Poynting theorem (standard); no retrieved open-code antenna benchmark.

## 5. Differentiable
**Model:** pathwise gradients for Nanbu angles and Niel noise (stop-gradient on pairing indices, implicit-root derivative for `A(s)`), no AD through MC photon emission; chunked `jax.checkpoint` with `scan`; explicit integrator for reverse mode (implicit uses while_loop); windowed horizon. **Alternatives:** score-function (Arya 2022) for discrete events, CRN finite differences as oracle.
**Targets:** AD vs CRN-FD of an expectation within sampling error; AD vs analytic derivative of the coth solution; noise floor vs N.
**Sources:** JAX-in-Cell docs/arXiv 2512.12160; Carvalho 2026 arXiv 2601.10885; Arya 2022; JAX checkpointing docs. No prior differentiable-binary-collision PIC found.
