# Step 3: phase decorrelation of the comb, and where DIII-D sits

Scripts in `scripts/`, data in `data/` (one npz per run, logs `map.log`, `checks.log`), figures in `figures/`. Nothing outside `results/step3/` was touched, apart from an appended section in PLAN.md. Laptop only, 4 single-thread workers, machine load 100-250 from other jobs.

## Verdict
The hypothesis is **supported in its qualitative form, with two corrections**.
1. Sustained (unbounded) quasilinear growth needs phase decorrelation: with Gamma_phi = 0 the late growth rate is zero at every sigma (0.5-4); with random phase it turns on at Gamma_phi/Delta_omega of about 0.01-0.03 and reaches the QL rate at about 0.1-0.3, i.e. a coherence time 1/Gamma_phi of order one to a few beat periods. The dependence on sigma is weak above sigma = 0.5; overlap (sigma > 1) mainly sets how fast the comb band fills (3-300 beat periods), not whether growth is sustained.
2. **Correction to step 1.** In a finite comb band, the late-time slope of <dg^2> vanishes for ordinary QL diffusion too (particles leave the line band and stop). "D_eff/D_QL < 0.03 after 1e3 periods" therefore does not by itself prove non-QL behaviour. What distinguishes the coherent comb is the plateau: it fills to 0.5-0.7 of complete band mixing (fill = <dg^2>/(Lw^2/6) = 0.54-0.69 at sigma >= 1, three seeds) and stays there to 3000 periods, whereas any Gamma_phi >= 0.01 Delta_omega goes through fill 1 and keeps growing. Also, the coherent comb fills the band quickly (0.5 of complete mixing in 266, 13, 5, 1 beat periods at sigma = 1, 1.5, 2, 3), at 1-3 times the QL rate (s_k below).

## Model and method
`scripts/pn.py`: the step-1 stationary reduced model (`red_st.py` right-hand side, tangential term kappa = 1, RK4), N = 10 equal lines (eqN10, Delta k = 0.57 /m, Delta_omega = 1.67e8 rad/s, beat period 37.6 ns), no drive or collisions, particles uniform over the line band, window T = 1000 beat periods (3000 in the check), dtfac 0.1 (step set by the fastest line rate; same definition as step 1).
Noise: each line j has phase phi_j(t) = phi_j0 + W_j(t), W_j an independent random walk with <dW^2> = 2 Gamma_phi t (Lorentzian line, HWHM Gamma_phi, field coherence exp(-Gamma_phi |tau|)). Every particle has its own noise realisation (non-interacting particles, so the ensemble is also over realisations). The noise phase is advanced once per RK4 step. Gamma_phi/Delta_omega = 0, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3. The Chirikov values sigma (nominal 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4) are the step-1 definition (effective 0.51, 1.02, 1.53, 2.04, 3.06, 4.1).
Ensemble: 3 seeds (line phases, initial conditions, noise) x 64 particles per cell = 192 particles, 126 runs. Errors are the standard error over the 3 seeds (crude).

Because the band is finite, the slope of <dg^2> is not a usable single number for all cells. Three quantities are reported (`scripts/analyze.py`):
- **fill** = <dg^2>(T)/(Lw^2/6), last 10% of the window. QL diffusion confined to the band tends to 1; the coherent plateau is 0.5-0.7.
- **s_late**: growth rate of <dg^2> over [T/2, T] divided by the quasilinear Fokker-Planck prediction at the same displacement (`scripts/qlref.py`: dense comb smoothed to Lorentzian width max(Gamma_phi, Delta_omega), D = D_QL inside, finite band with wings, 1 = QL, 0 = bounded). It is only trustworthy where fill is below about 2 (reference domain +-3 band widths); for larger fill the particles have been heated far outside the band by the Lorentzian wings of the noisy lines and the number is not meaningful (cells with Gamma_phi >= 0.3 at sigma >= 1.5 are unreliable).
- **s_k**: mixing-equivalent D_eff/D_QL, the ratio of the QL-FP time to the simulated time for <dg^2> to go from 0.1 to 0.5 of Lw^2/6 (evaluated at T if 0.5 is not reached: sigma = 0.5, an upper bound).

### Limits checked
- **Gamma_phi >> Delta gives D_QL:** only a weak check. At sigma = 0.5 (fill below 0.4, no saturation) the late slope over [T/2, T] divided by D_QL is 1.2 +- 0.6, 1.6 +- 0.4, 1.2 +- 0.4 at Gamma_phi/Delta = 0.3, 1, 3 (3 seeds; heavy-tailed, seeds range 0.1-2.0), consistent with 1 but not better than about 40%. For sigma >= 1 the Lorentzian wings make the band-based comparison ambiguous (see above). I did not run enough particles to tighten it.
- **Gamma_phi = 0 reproduces step 1:** late-half slope/D_QL = 0.000-0.005 at sigma = 1, 1.5 for T = 1000 and 3000; plateau fill 0.54-0.69, in the step-1 range 0.1-0.6 (a little higher at sigma = 2-3, 0.62-0.69; step 1 had 0.22 at sigma = 2.2 with 32 particles).
- **Window independence (T = 1000 vs 3000, 1 seed x 64 particles, `scripts/conv.py`):** raw late slope/D_QL at sigma = 1.5: 0.000/-0.001 (Gamma = 0), 0.010/0.000 (0.01), 0.017/0.014 (0.03), 0.050/0.034 (0.1); at sigma = 1: 0.005/0.006, 0.038/0.037, 0.083/0.016, -0.012/0.104. The coherent value stays zero (fill 0.62 -> 0.64, 0.56 -> 0.52); the noisy cases keep filling (fill 1.01 -> 1.51, 1.33 -> 2.15, 1.66 -> 3.84 at sigma = 1.5). Slopes agree within the noise (about +-0.04); the cell-to-cell noise of this check is too large to say more.
- **dt convergence (dtfac 0.1 vs 0.05, T = 300, 1 seed x 64):** coherent cases agree (fill 0.66/0.63 and 0.95/0.71 at sigma = 1.5, 3, late slopes 0 within 0.04); noisy cases differ by up to a factor 2 in fill (0.65/1.32 at sigma = 1.5, Gamma 0.03; 2.4/1.7 at sigma = 3), which is the sampling noise of 64 particles with different noise streams (the Euler-Maruyama noise differs between step sizes), not a trend with dt: s_k at sigma = 1.5 is 2.9/3.1, 2.4/2.5, 2.0/2.4. The map is therefore good to a factor of about 1.5-2 per cell; the sign of every conclusion above (zero at Gamma_phi = 0, onset between 0.01 and 0.1, QL-scale at 0.1-0.3) is stable.

## Caveats
- Random-walk phase gives Lorentzian lines with 1/x^2 wings, which heat particles far outside the band (fill up to 5e4 at sigma = 4, Gamma = 3). A source with a cut-off noise spectrum would not. This does not affect the onset (Gamma_phi <= 0.1) but makes Gamma_phi >= 0.3 cells at sigma >= 1.5 uninterpretable beyond "diffusion continues".
- One comb (10 equal lines, Delta k = 0.57 /m), no E-field drift, no collisions, finite band. Sustained growth in a finite band is defined relative to the QL curve; there is no band-free measure here. Delta_omega scales with Delta k, so for a denser real k_par spectrum the Gamma_phi that matters scales with the smallest relevant line spacing.
- 3 seeds x 64 particles; the s_late errors are 30-100% in the transition cells.

## Physical Gamma_phi at DIII-D (`scripts/gphi_estimate.py`)
Inputs: f = 476 MHz klystron, 1 dB bandwidth +-1.5 MHz, 10 MHz antenna band (PPCF 68 105047, Sec 2.2-2.3); Delta_omega = 1.67e8 rad/s (beat 37.6 ns); step-2 damping Gamma_P median 2.7e5 /s (95%: 4e4-1.4e6), lifetime 3.7 us = 98 beat periods, about 70 chords; PPCF p12 broadband loop fluctuations during helicon-on (parametric decay, modes about 8 MHz apart); PPCF p25 "many passes ... stochasticized".
1. **Source linewidth.** The papers give no linewidth (the +-1.5 MHz is amplifier gain bandwidth). A phase-locked 476 MHz source has Hz to kHz linewidth: Gamma_phi/Delta_omega = 4e-8 to 4e-5. And a common phase noise on all lines does not decorrelate the comb. It matters only through the relative phase of components arriving with delay differences dtau (multipass, up to the 3.7 us lifetime): variance 2 Gamma_s dtau, which scrambles the lines only if Gamma_s > Gamma_P = 2.7e5 rad/s (43 kHz HWHM). Below that the multipass comb has random but frozen relative phases, which is exactly the coherent case. To reach Gamma_phi = 0.03 / 0.1 Delta_omega by source noise or FM would need HWHM of 0.8 / 2.7 MHz (2 pi f), i.e. the P3 "3-10 MHz FM" prediction gives 0.1-0.4.
2. **Multipass.** Gives a fixed set of random phases while the plasma is stationary, so it supplies the amplitudes and phases of the lines but no Gamma_phi.
3. **Plasma phase noise (my estimate, no measurement).** Phase per pass k_perp L (dn/n)/2 with k_perp = 70 /m, L = 1-2 m, dn/n = 0.3-3%: 0.1-2 rad. Turbulence correlation times 1-100 us are longer than or comparable to the 3.7 us life, so the phase is mostly frozen; Gamma_phi about phi^2/tau_c = 1e2-4e6 /s, i.e. Gamma_phi/Delta_omega = 1e-6 to 3e-2, nominal 1e-4 to 1e-3.
4. **Parametric decay.** The loop sees broadband fluctuations with 8 MHz spacing (2 pi 7.6 MHz = 0.29 Delta_omega). If the helicon pump is broadened or sideband-split at that scale the effective Gamma_phi could be up to 0.04-0.3 Delta_omega, but the loop signal is uncalibrated and the pump-sideband amplitude at the resonant location is unknown. This is the only mechanism in the papers that could put DIII-D in the sustained regime.
5. **Geometry (not a Gamma_phi, listed for completeness).** The comb is a toroidal-mode comb of a finite, frozen set of (n, m) modes; a real field line sees a denser k_par spectrum (Delta n = 1, many m), which lowers Delta_omega and raises sigma at fixed power. That changes the map coordinates, not the coherence. I could not quantify it from the papers.

## DIII-D on the (b, Gamma_phi) map
- b: step 2 gives b/b_thr medians 0.39 (43 kW), 0.52 (76 kW), 0.69 (150 kW). With sigma ~ sqrt(b/b_thr) (sigma = 1 at b_thr), the median sigma is 0.62, 0.72, 0.83; P(sigma > 1) = 6%, 17%, 24%. 
- Gamma_phi/Delta_omega: nominal 1e-5 to 1e-3 (source plus turbulence), with a not excluded upper range to 0.04-0.3 only through parametric-decay or deliberate FM.
- So DIII-D sits at sigma 0.6-0.8, Gamma_phi/Delta_omega <~ 1e-3: bottom-left corner, sub-overlap and coherent, where the map gives zero sustained scattering (s_late 0.00 at sigma 0.5-1; 0.01-0.06 is the noise at Gamma = 0). Even the upper tail P(sigma > 1) of 6-24% would be coherent, hence bounded, unless Gamma_phi >~ 0.03 Delta_omega (1 MHz equivalent) is present. The helicon at its present settings is expected to scatter by the bounded passage kicks only (step 1: coherent drag/kicks of 3-15 keV per passage below threshold), not by sustained QL diffusion.
- Consequence: the existing DIII-D effect (HXR reduction above 7 MeV at 76 kW) is not explained by sustained QL scattering of a coherent comb; either Gamma_phi is much larger than estimated (the parametric-decay route), the amplitude is above threshold with bounded but large band-filling kicks plus E-field refill (not tested here), or the mechanism is something else. Testable: FM of 1-3 MHz at fixed power (predicted to turn sustained scattering on), a DBS measurement of the pump linewidth and of the sideband amplitude.
