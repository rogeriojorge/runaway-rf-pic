# runaway_rf_minimum

Best-found minimum supplied RF energy to keep a runaway tail below 10 MeV: scenario, quasilinear whistler response validated against resolved pushes, and an explicit source-energy ledger. The default `stages` list runs scenario, validate and source. The optimization, verification and margin stages need `out_dir` and take hours in the research setting. See docs/runaway_rf_control.md.

    python studies/runaway_rf_minimum/runaway_rf_minimum.py

Quick preset, about 10 s.
